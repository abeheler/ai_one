Cheat Sheet Shortcut

Debit (Dr): Latin "debere" → "Left side"

Credit (Cr): Latin "credere" → "Right side"

⚠️ They do NOT mean positive, negative, good, or bad!

Step 1: The Great Myth Buster

Beginner Level

When you look at your phone and see Credit Card Refund Received, you think credits are awesome. When your bank Debited your account for monthly fees, you think debits are terrible.

"In professional accounting, we throw that context away. Debit (Dr) simply means LEFT SIDE of a ledger. Credit (Cr) simply means RIGHT SIDE."

Interactive Myth-Buster Check

"Debits always decrease accounts and credits always increase accounts."

"The terms Debit and Credit represent simple physical directions on a ledger sheet."

Step 2: The Accounting Equation Scale

Core Theory

The entire business world is built on the Accounting Equation. Every single transaction must keep this scale perfectly balanced:

✨ The Accounting Scale is perfectly balanced!

Rule of Thumb: Accounts on the Left side of the equation (Assets) normally have a Debit balance. Accounts on the Right side (Liabilities & Equity) normally have a Credit balance.

Step 3: The DEALER Rule Guide

Core Formula

To expand our understanding beyond just Assets and Liabilities, we use the golden industry acronym: D-E-A-L-E-R.

D.E.A. NORMAL BALANCE: DEBIT (LEFT)
Dividends / Drawings Dr+ / Cr-
Expenses Dr+ / Cr-
Assets Dr+ / Cr-

Adding to these accounts requires writing a DEBIT on the left side.

L.E.R. NORMAL BALANCE: CREDIT (RIGHT)
Liabilities Dr- / Cr+
Equity (Common Stock) Dr- / Cr+
Revenue / Income Dr- / Cr+

Adding to these accounts requires writing a CREDIT on the right side.
